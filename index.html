HOW TO PLAY SHORT CIRCUIT

⚡ LIVE vs. DUD WIRES

At the start of every round, the console displays the exact total wire pool (e.g. 3 Live Wires and 2 Dud Wires).

The wires are secretly randomized into the chamber. As wires are triggered or snipped, the pool counters update in real time.

🎲 THE SELF-SHOCK GAMBIT

On your turn, you have two primary choices:

  • Shock Opponent: Pick a rival target! If Live, deals damage and passes turn. If Dud, no damage and passes turn.
  • Shock Yourself: If Dud, you suffer no damage AND earn an extra turn immediately! If Live, you take damage and your turn ends.

🧰 TOOL ITEMS & STRATEGY

1–2 items are added to your toolbox each round (max 4). You can ONLY use items on your turn:

📟 Multimeter: Secretly scan if the next wire is Live or Dud.
✂️ Wire Cutters: Safely clip out the next wire without discharge.
⚡ Voltage Booster: Doubles damage of next Live wire to 2 HP!
🧤 Insulated Glove: Ground rival, skipping next player's turn.
🔁 Circuit Tap: Recycles current wire to the back of chamber.
📡 Signal Jammer: Blocks next rival from using items on their turn.
